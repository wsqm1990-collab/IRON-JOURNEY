# Iron Journey Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Iron Journey is a strength and cardio training app with an artificial intelligence (AI) coach and an RPG journey. This policy explains, in plain words, what data the app uses, why, where it stays and how you control it.

## 1. Who looks after your data

The person responsible for your data (the "controller", in the words of Brazil's data protection law, the LGPD) is the developer of Iron Journey, based in Brazil. To reach the developer, use the contact email shown on the app's page on Google Play or the App Store.

## 2. What data the app uses

- **Profile:** name or nickname, goal, level, how many days and how long you train, sex, age, weight, height, body measurements (waist, neck, hips), any pain or injuries you mention, and what you like or prefer to avoid.
- **Gym:** the equipment and weights you have available.
- **Workouts and cardio:** exercises, sets, loads, reps, times, distances, effort, any heart rate you type in, and your notes.
- **Conversations with the coach:** the questions you ask and the answers.
- **Form videos and photos:** only if you use the form check. Your phone takes a few still frames from the video, and the app does not keep the video.
- **Journey character:** look, name and the reward you choose.

Weight, body measurements, pain, injuries and heart rate are **health data**, which the LGPD treats as sensitive. The app only uses them with your consent and only to build, adjust and follow your training.

## 3. Where your data stays

Your data stays **on your phone**. The app has no sign-up, no account and no cloud of its own for your workouts. Your phone's system may include the app's data in its automatic Google (Android) or iCloud (iPhone) backup, depending on your settings. You can also save a copy as a file in Settings.

## 4. Artificial intelligence

When you use an AI feature (building or adjusting your plan, talking to the coach, asking about an exercise, the technique lesson, a form check or the body assessment), the app sends **Anthropic**, the company behind the Claude AI, only what that answer needs. For example: your profile, your plan, your latest workouts and your question. A form check sends a few frames from the video.

The data travels encrypted, either straight from the app to Anthropic or through our server, which does not keep the content of questions or answers. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, Anthropic may not use this data to train AI models.

## 5. What the app does not do

- It does not sell your data.
- It does not show ads.
- It does not use advertising tracking.
- It does not share your data with anyone besides Anthropic (for the AI). Once subscriptions exist, Google Play or the App Store handles the payment, and a subscription service (RevenueCat) receives only the purchase details. The app never sees your card details.

The app also checks GitHub for updates, because that is where they are published. GitHub receives technical data with that check, such as the phone's IP address.

## 6. Phone permissions

- **Notifications:** alerts from the timer, rest periods and guided workouts.
- **Running in the background:** the timer and the voice keep going while you use another app, such as a streaming or music app.
- **Camera and gallery:** only when you choose to film or pick a video for the form check.
- **Video and music apps:** the app checks whether some of these apps are installed, so it can open them directly when you tap the button.

## 7. Your rights

Under the LGPD you can confirm what data exists, see it, correct it, take it elsewhere and delete it, and you can withdraw your consent. Since almost everything stays on your phone, you do this yourself in the app:

- **Correct:** edit your profile.
- **Take it with you:** save a copy as a file in Settings.
- **Delete:** tap "Delete my data" in Settings. This deletes everything the app stored on the device and also withdraws your consent.

For any question, write to the contact email.

## 8. Age

Iron Journey is for people aged 18 or over.

## 9. Security

The data on your phone is protected by the phone's own system, and everything that leaves the app is encrypted. If you use your own AI key, it stays only on your phone and is left out of backups.

## 10. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, this page will be updated, and important changes will be announced in the app.
